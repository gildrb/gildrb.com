# T3

![](media:t3-canvas-sketches)

## Several products

Hundreds of versions of a self-initiated T3 logomark, drawn in public from March to May 2026.

T3 has more than one product. The mark must work as t3.chat, as t3.code, and alone in a favicon or an app grid. Nobody commissioned it.

## One spine

The crossbar of the T continues into the 3, so the pair reads as one figure. I drew hundreds of versions to find how far the 3 can open and how much of the T can go.

![](media:t3-canvas-overview)
