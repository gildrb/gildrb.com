# mL7

A channel logo for the Overwatch player Mihai “mL7” Lupascu.

![](media:ml7-logo-system)

## A thank-you

In 2018 I played competitive Overwatch and learned from better players. One of them was Mihai “mL7” Lupascu. I made a logo for him as a thank-you. The logo had to stay clear in small stream overlays.

## The mark

I drew a compact geometric mL. A brush stroke through the 7 gives the mark its direction. The letters are black and the stroke is orange. I changed the spacing and the stroke angle until the logo worked as a profile image and as a wide channel graphic.

## His channel

His manager contacted me, and the mark became the identity of his channel. Mihai paid for work that I did not bill. In July 2026 his Twitch channel still uses the mark, in a different colorway.
