# About Gil Rodrigues (gildrb)

Gil Rodrigues is a designer based in Germany. Online he goes by gildrb; his full name is Gil Domingos Rodrigues Barbosa, and Gil Rodrigues Barbosa refers to the same person.

## This site

gildrb.com is his portfolio. Each project on the [homepage](/) links to a case study that walks through the work, including the directions that were set aside along the way. The [all projects](/all) page shows every case study on one page.
