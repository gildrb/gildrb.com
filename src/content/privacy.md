# Privacy

gildrb.com is a personal portfolio. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking, or advertising scripts, and it collects no personal data of its own.

Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser’s local storage (`localStorage`), and your scroll position is kept in session storage so the back button returns you to the same place. Both stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
