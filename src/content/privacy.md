# Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking or advertising scripts.

Your light or dark theme choice, and your scroll position for back navigation, are kept in your browser's storage. Neither is sent anywhere.

The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
