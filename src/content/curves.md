# CURVES

![](media:curves-demo)

## The question

A free display typeface with 96 unicase glyphs, built from geometric forms.

In 2019 I drew logos from geometric shapes. I wanted to make a full character set with one construction rule.

![](media:curves-wordmark)

![](media:curves-letterforms)

## Every glyph

Each glyph uses flat stems and quarter turns at one radius. Where the rule leaves a gap, the gap stays, so the R breaks at the shoulder and the Q keeps an open corner. Uppercase and lowercase share one drawing. The zero has a slash, so it is different from the O.

I released CURVES on DaFont in 2019, free for personal and commercial use. By August 2026 it had more than 23,000 downloads.
