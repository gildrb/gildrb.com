# Ben Davis

A db monogram for the developer Ben Davis, and the site that it lives on.

![](media:ben-davis-brandmark)

## The old mark

Ben Davis builds software and co-hosts the Nerd Snipe podcast. His old mark set a slab-sided d against a round b. The two letters did not share a weight or a curve.

![](media:ben-davis-original)

## One diagonal

A d and a b are mirror images. I kept that symmetry. One diagonal carries the stem of the d into the bowl of the b. The stems and bowls have one weight and one corner radius. The mark is one path.

![](media:ben-davis-construction)

## In use

I posted the mark in July 2026 and sent it to Ben. He replied, and the mark is his to use. I designed and built davis7.sh around it. On the site, the db turns into the wordmark.
